Research proposal · speculative decoding for reasoning models · 4-page workshop target

Phase-structured entropy in chain-of-thought: a state, or just a smoothed level?

Is there a latent reasoning-phase state in chain-of-thought entropy that predicts speculative-decoding acceptance beyond instantaneous confidence?

TL;DR

Per-token entropy along a long chain-of-thought is bimodal and looks organized into phases: low-entropy “execution” runs broken by short high-entropy “decision” bursts. We ask the one question that decides whether this matters for inference — does a latent phase state, inferred from the whole trajectory, carry information about accepted-run length beyond the instantaneous drafter confidence and its causal smoothing that current adaptive methods already use? On the way, we settle a standing contradiction in the literature. The measurement is the contribution; the controller is a bounded demonstration.

01 — Abstract

▾Abstract

Speculative decoding (SD) accelerates LLM inference losslessly, and a large 2024–2026 literature now sets draft length and tree width from the drafter's instantaneous entropy or confidence. We ask whether long chain-of-thought reasoning traces contain something more: a latent phase state that predicts SD acceptance beyond what the instantaneous signal already provides. We contribute, in order of how defensible each is:

C1
Test whether per-token CoT entropy is bimodal and segments into contiguous phases — using the trace as the unit and autocorrelation-preserving nulls.
C2 · safe
Reconcile the +0.56 / −0.18 entropy↔acceptance contradiction by decomposing it along two axes prior work conflated. Holds regardless of “phases.”
C3 · core
The state-vs-level test: does a latent phase state add incremental predictive value after conditioning on instantaneous confidence and a bandwidth-matched smoothing?
C4 · demo
If signal remains, how much a training-free causal detector recovers — at matched FLOPs and wall-clock, stacked on EAGLE-3.

We pre-register the falsifiers, including that the honest outcome of C3 may be “level, not state.” We release the corpus and analysis.

02 — Motivation

▾Why this question, and why now

Reasoning models emit very long CoT before a short answer, so decode latency dominates and SD is the natural remedy. The right draft length and tree shape should depend on where in its reasoning the model is.

During a determined computation — apply a formula, copy an intermediate result — the next tokens are predictable and a drafter can race ahead. At a decision — which lemma, whether to branch, when to stop — the distribution spreads and hedging across candidates beats committing to a long chain.

The reasoning-science literature documents the structure: a minority of high-entropy “forking” tokens carry decisions while the majority are low-entropy followers (Beyond-80/20); thinking phases have higher entropy than answer phases (PEAR); CoT looks like a transition between exploratory and structured regimes (EDRM). None of it links the structure to inference. Meanwhile the SD literature already uses the instantaneous entropy to set draft length (AdaEDL, SVIP) and joint depth+width trees (HeteroSpec, TALON, SAGE, GOOSE). So the open question is sharper than “does entropy predict acceptance.”

Is the phase phenomenon a state, or just a level seen through a low-pass filter?

If a token's acceptance prospects are fully captured by its instantaneous predictability and a short causal smoothing of it, then “phases” are just a level, memoryless control is already near-optimal, and the field should stop adding state machines. If instead there is a latent state — inferable from the whole trajectory's dynamics but not the instantaneous level alone — it carries information a causal detector is leaving on the table. The answer is not obvious either way, and we design to resolve it rather than to confirm a hoped-for direction.

Why the question isn't circular: a causal threshold of a smoothed signal cannot, by the data-processing inequality, carry information the smoothed signal lacks — so we do not define the scientific phase that way. We define it as a latent state inferred by a hidden-state model with access to the full past-and-future trajectory; such a state is not a function of the causal conditioning set, so incremental information is possible.

03 — Research & practical impact

▾What this buys the field, and a deployment

Research impact
  • A correctly-powered, autocorrelation-aware test of whether CoT entropy is bimodal and phase-structured — statistics, not histograms.
  • The draft-vs-target × within-vs-across reconciliation of a live contradiction, useful to anyone designing entropy-based controllers and true regardless of our phase result.
  • A clean state-vs-level verdict that tells the community whether hidden-state machinery is worth building.
  • A reusable corpus annotated with draft/target per-token entropy, latent phase labels, position, tree depth, and accept/reject outcomes.
Practical / business impact
  • Reasoning-model serving is costly because outputs are long; any lossless decode speedup is a direct cost/latency win with no quality to defend.
  • If a state exists and a cheap causal detector recovers a useful fraction, the result is a training-free controller that layers on the production-standard drafter (EAGLE-3) with a few interpretable thresholds.
  • If it does not, the field is saved the effort of building one — also valuable.
  • We report the controller's gain at matched FLOPs and wall-clock and stacked on EAGLE-3, so the practical claim is honest about deployment.
04 — Background & current approaches

▾The ground we build on

New to speculative decoding? The companion page explains all of this from scratch with animations. In brief:

Lossless SD

A drafter proposes tokens; the target verifies in one pass and accepts a prefix via modified rejection sampling (accept with probability min(1, p/q); on reject resample from normalized (p−q)₊), preserving the target's exact distribution (Leviathan et al. 2023; Chen et al. 2023). Verification generalizes to token trees (SpecInfer; EAGLE-2 dynamic tree; Sequoia DP-optimal trees). EAGLE-2's tree already runs deep where draft confidence is high — a phase-reactive behavior any phase-state method must out-perform, not merely match.

Memoryless entropy control already exists and is strong

AdaEDL stops when 1 − √(γ·H_DM) falls below a threshold; SVIP derives the same Pinsker bound and reports +22% on QwQ at 8K. SpecKV reports the instantaneous draft-entropy↔acceptance correlation at ρ≈+0.56 — a strong pointwise predictor. HeteroSpec (2505.13254) stratifies cumulative entropy to jointly tune depth and pruning width, training-free and EAGLE-compatible (~5.36× in long low-entropy runs) — the closest memoryless joint controller, and ~8 months prior to TALON. TALON, SAGE, GOOSE all do joint depth+width from the instantaneous signal. DSDE uses KLD-variance as a “regional stability” signal — the closest in spirit to phase persistence.

The contradiction we resolve

SpecKV's +0.56 (draft entropy, within a single setting) and Acceptance Dynamics' −0.18 (target entropy, across cognitive domains) point in opposite directions. The difference is plausibly which entropy and within vs across trace — exactly the decomposition no one has run. Attention Drift adds a third, attention-level null. AdaEAGLE already quantifies a ~29% oracle gap for adaptive length alone, so any phase-oracle headroom must be reported net of what adaptive-length oracles already capture.

Reasoning SD (orthogonal / stackable)

SpecReason, Lookahead-Reasoning, RSD and SemanticSpec (“Beyond Tokens”, 2602.03708; lossless, R1-32B/QwQ-32B, 2.1–2.7×, probes internal states) operate at step/semantic granularity. SemanticSpec is also the precedent for our analysis-only hidden-state probe.

05 — Limitations of prior work

▾The gap we exploit

  1. The bimodal/phase claim is asserted, never tested under autocorrelation. Histograms and qualitative two-population descriptions exist (Beyond-80/20, PEAR; LESS even notes “contiguous low-entropy segments”), but no bimodality test with an autocorrelation-preserving null, no run-length characterization, no separation of marginal shape from temporal structure.
  2. The draft-vs-target, within-vs-across decomposition has not been run, leaving the +0.56 / −0.18 contradiction unresolved.
  3. No one has tested whether a latent state (vs the instantaneous level) carries incremental acceptance information. Existing methods all condition on the instantaneous signal; whether trajectory-level state adds anything is unmeasured.
  4. Adaptive control is memoryless. AdaEDL/SVIP/HeteroSpec/TALON/SAGE/GOOSE react to the instantaneous signal; DSDE uses variance but for length only. The oracle-vs-causal gap for a phase state is unmeasured.

The exploitable gap is (2)+(3): the reconciliation, and the state-vs-level test — both unoccupied, both sign-robust in value.

The controller mechanism (limitation 4) is occupied and is therefore a demonstration, not a headline.

06 — Proposed idea / approach

▾One signal, four contributions

Per token we log the drafter's full next-token distribution and entropy (and top-1 prob), and the target's entropy. The pipeline runs on drafter entropy as primary (deployable) and target entropy in parallel (for the reconciliation). Click a stage to see what it does.

C1 — Phase structure, tested correctly

Unit = the trace. Per-trace Hartigan's dip judged against an autocorrelation-preserving null (stationary/block bootstrap with Politis–White block length; an AR/single-state-HMM surrogate matching the empirical autocorrelation), reporting the dip-statistic distribution, the fraction of traces rejecting unimodality, and n_eff. Two distinct sub-hypotheses: marginal bimodality (GMM/BIC, Silverman) and two-phase temporal structure (2-state HMM / PELT beats a memoryless model; run-lengths vs a geometric null). Artifact controls: re-run with formatting/cue tokens masked, and within a single homogeneous <think> span.

C2 — The reconciliation sign-robust headline

Under a single fixed (L,B) configuration, estimate the entropy↔acceptance relationship in a 2×2 design: {draft, target} entropy × {within-trace, across-trace}, controlling for position and tree depth. The hypothesis: draft-entropy-within-trace is the strongly-predictive cell (reproducing SpecKV's ~0.56) while target-entropy-across-domain is the weak/negative cell (reproducing Acceptance Dynamics' −0.18). This stands whether or not any latent phase state exists — which is why it anchors the paper.

C3 — The state-vs-level test the core question

  • The latent state. The scientific phase label is the posterior state of a 2-state HMM fit by forward–backward — it uses the whole trajectory, so it is not a deterministic function of the causal instantaneous signal and can carry incremental information. (A causal threshold of a smoothing — the deployable detector — provably cannot; we use it only for C4.)
  • Measurement under fixed drafting. SD is run with a single phase-agnostic (L,B) so accepted-run length is measured under identical caps; we run several L values and report the censored fraction per phase.
  • The conditional test. A censoring-aware discrete-time survival model (cloglog link; the L-cap as censoring) for accepted-run length, with per-trace random effects, a spline in position, and tree depth as covariates. Nested predictors: (a) instantaneous confidence/entropy + the AdaEDL bound; (b) + a bandwidth-matched causal EWMA of entropy; (c) + the latent HMM phase state. The state matters only if it adds significant incremental value over both (a) and (b) — a time-dependent C-index with CIs and LR tests under multiplicity correction.
  • Drafter-blind confirmation (analysis-only). We test whether a phase label from signals the drafter does not see — a lexical-cue detector (training-free) and an offline probe on target hidden states (explicitly analysis-only; SemanticSpec precedent) — also predicts acceptance. We earn the word “state” only if a drafter-blind label also predicts; absent that, we report the weaker, still-honest claim.

C4 — Controller bounded demonstration

A training-free causal detector (EWMA of draft entropy with a hysteresis band) drives a small interpretable phase→(depth/width split) policy under a fixed node budget. All hyperparameters are fit on calibration, selected on validation, and frozen before any test number. We report the oracle-vs-online gap: how much of the acausal HMM-state's value the causal detector recovers — net of an adaptive-length oracle, and over EAGLE-3 and HeteroSpec, not just vanilla.

Losslessness

A proof obligation that dynamic (L,B) leaves the accept/resample rule unchanged; direct verification of the sampler invariant on logged (p,q); a TOST equivalence test (pre-registered tolerance ε = the Monte-Carlo CI width at M resamples) with Holm (FWER) control across positions; greedy behavioral equality; and an EQSPEC ragged-tensor check at batch>1.

09 — Expected results

▾What would confirm or falsify each claim

HypothesisExpected outcome
H1 structureMajority of traces reject unimodality vs the autocorrelation-preserving null; 2-state temporal model wins; survives masking and the within-<think> restriction. Else falsified.
H2 expected to holdThe 2×2 cleanly separates draft-within (strong +) from target-across (weak −), resolving the contradiction. The result we are most confident in; it anchors the paper.
H3 genuinely uncertain — the pointEither the latent state adds significant incremental C-index (→ “a real phase state exists; build causal detectors”) or it does not (→ “level, not state; memoryless-on-smoothed-entropy is near-optimal”). Both are publishable; we are powered to tell them apart.
H4 controllerIf H3 is positive, a causal detector recovers a positive, FLOP/wall-clock-matched fraction of the oracle gain; we report the oracle-vs-online gap and the EAGLE-3-stacked number, even if small or null.
