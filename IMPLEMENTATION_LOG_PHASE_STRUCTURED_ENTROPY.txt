P2.A.3 Autocorrelation-preserving null checks:
- block-bootstrap Dip null: no robust marginal bimodality confirmation
- AR surrogate Dip: mixed significance
- single-state HMM surrogate Dip: mixed

Interpretation:
- Marginal bimodality weakens under autocorrelation-preserving nulls.
- Temporal phase organization is strong and persistent.
- Entropy dynamics are phase-structured in time rather than memoryless IID draws.

H1 verdict:
- Phase structure confirmed.
- Strict marginal bimodality not robustly confirmed.
